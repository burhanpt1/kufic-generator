Letter alternates

Phase 6.6. Long forms of final noon, yeh, kaf and meem, designed for the cell grid. Raised tails curl back and stay below an ascender, so they never read as alif; meem's tail descends. With alternates on, the justifier gives up to 60% of a line's slack to them, spread along the line, instead of stretching everything. With no room, lines are identical to the plain set.
