Taste trainer

Lab port §3. The studio's Trainer tab shows one composition at a time to rate (Veto 3/←, Meh 2/↓, Like 1/→, Skip space). Two logistic regressions learn from the ratings: a veto model on traits alone, which screens genomes before they are rendered, and a liking model on traits plus the critic measures. Here 60 ratings are simulated from the pattern the owner's lab ratings showed: hearts and teardrops vetoed, heavy strokes vetoed, circles and polygons liked, stars and mihrabs mixed, vetoes with more empty space.
