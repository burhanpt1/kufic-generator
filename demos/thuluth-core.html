Thuluth core (TypeScript port)

src/script/thuluth: the lab's pen, letter set, shaping, joining and kashida, with the lab's globals as one explicit style and no DOM. Its line output is identical to the lab's, byte for byte, for every reference. A small pure rasteriser replaces the browser canvas the lab used to measure ink.
