Band headroom

Tall letters used to fill the whole band, so stems ran into the next separator. Each text band now keeps empty rows above and below its letters: at least half a cell beyond any ink (stem endings such as leaves and palmettes rise above their stems, so they get more), and the tallest ink ends at about 86% of the band. The band grows; the outline grows or the letters shrink to keep the text whole. Left: before. Right: now.
