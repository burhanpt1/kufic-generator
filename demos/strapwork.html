Strapwork fill

Lab port §4. The text runs along the strands of a Hankin star pattern (8-fold at 67°, 6- and 12-fold at 60°, square at 45°), which weave over and under: each strap's edges break where it passes under another. Strands run on past the region's edge and are clipped; text sits only on straight pieces well inside, clear of crossings and corners, with half a cell of strap above and below the letters. The pattern scale is searched so the text uses most of the straps it is given; spare straps stay plain.
