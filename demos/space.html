Empty space

Lab port §2. Each composition is rasterised at 160×160 straight from its placed geometry (workers can't decode SVG): letters, lines and ornaments as ink, pattern and vine areas as texture. On a 40×40 grid, flat paper-light cells count as blank, ignoring a 3-cell frame. The score falls when more than 30% is blank or when the largest enclosed blank patch passes 5% (the red cells). It joins the critic as “Little empty space” with weight 0.3.
