Phase 5: the studio

The interface over the composition model, three levels deep:

  • Gallery: the eight presets, Breed (pick one or two, lock traits from a pick, vary the current composition) and favourites. Picks teach a taste that is kept in this browser.
  • Editor: text slots with live abjad values, guided controls (shape, borders, centre, letters, style, counting conventions, an outline from any SVG file), and a layers panel to add, remove, reorder and edit nodes as JSON.
  • Chronogram: read a phrase, complete it to a year (Gregorian, Hijri or a number), or find phrases from a word bank; Mashriqi or Maghribi values and ة as 5 or 400.
  • Letters: the glyph editor; edits apply to every composition.

The composition lives in the URL, so every state is a shareable link. Export as SVG or PNG. Open the studio on its own page.
