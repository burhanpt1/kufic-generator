Ali Asghar

The name in a thuluth cartouche framed by ornament alone (a braid between pearls and rules) on eight-fold Iznik tiles to the edge of an A3 sheet. Side by side; أصغر nested in the swoop of علي's ya with the ra swooping too; and أصغر stacked on the drawn-out join of علي's lam and ya (the best three of thirty seeds of each). npm run ali-asghar writes the print files.
