Lab fixes and the owner's feedback

1a. Rings on tight curves

A ring isn't placed where any of its stretches curves tighter than 1.25 band heights (measured away from corners); the solver tries smaller letters or stops adding rings. Captions give each ring's tightest radius over its band height.

1b–1c. Ranges and the flow fill

  • Evolvable stroke weight 0.55–0.85 (no 0.9 or 1.0), softness up to 0.3, size spread up to 1.3.
  • The flow fill is no longer evolved or offered in the guided controls; it stays in the layers panel's fill menu.

1d. Tughra strokes that grow out of the letters

The arm continues the final letter's tail, each flag rises from a stem top, and each loop leaves the word's free right end and comes back into the baseline along it; every join is smooth, with the letters' own width (and nib, in pen mode).
