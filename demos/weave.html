Interlaced maze

Phase 6.4. Ribbons of text on a knot lattice, in the manner of an orthogonal Celtic knot: at each node the ribbons cross straight through, alternately over and under, or turn back on rounded corners. Text sits on the straight stretches long enough for a word; the ribbon edges are drawn as walls, broken where a ribbon passes under another, so letters never overlap. The corridor setting is the chance of a crossing.
