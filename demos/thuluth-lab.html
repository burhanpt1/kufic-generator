Thuluth lab (vendored)

The thuluth lab as it arrived (prototypes/thuluth-lab/): a pen-stroke engine, not a font. Letters are skeletons drawn with a broad nib at 69°; the letter set is master fits over Calliar-learned forms over hand-drawn ones. This page runs the lab's own code; the repo's TypeScript port is checked against the references below.
