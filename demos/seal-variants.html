Taller seal letters

Lab port §6. Besides taller ascenders and wider stretches, each seal letter now has a variant 3 rows taller, made by repeating one row of its body: a row whose inked cells all have letter ink directly above and below and none beside (the middle such row). It costs 0.8 per added row in the search; letters with no such row (م) get no variant. The variants go into the tables that both the WebAssembly kernel and the JS fallback read.

Letters: as drawn, and 3 rows taller (the repeated row in the accent colour)
