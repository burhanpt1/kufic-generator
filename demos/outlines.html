Shapes and line heights

Phase 6.1 and 6.2. Any SVG silhouette can be a region's outline, at any depth: borders follow it and fills take its inside. Line fills can vary their height: each line's letter scale follows the shape's width at that height, so the narrow parts (an arch, a tip) get smaller lines instead of none.
