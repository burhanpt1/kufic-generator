Thuluth legibility

Rules the engine now keeps, each a mistake caught by eye in the lab (tests/thuluth-legibility.test.ts): every letter has its dots; vowels stay off the letters and none are dropped; confusable letters stay apart; loop letters keep their counters; no small loop where a bowl leaves a letter.

Final and isolated kaf, rebuilt

The lab's final kaf was a leaning shaft over a short foot, so بارك read as بارد or بارل. It now has an upright under a shoulder stroke, a long base that lifts at its end, and the small kaf sign inside.

Vowels under deep tails

A kasra under a final mim or nun used to be dropped when the tail filled the band below. It now sits under the letter's body beside the tail, and a crowded dagger alif searches further before giving up (never further than its own letter).
