Quasi-periodic tilings

De Bruijn's multigrid, from the thuluth lab: rhomb tilings with exact 8-, 10- and 12-fold symmetry that never repeat. They are now a source for both scripts: the star-pattern tilings mg8, mg10 and mg12, with Hankin's polygons-in-contact drawn on the rhombs, and Iznik tilework (cobalt vines, saz leaves, hatayi flowers) as a fill, a band ground, or the corners of a frame.

Iznik dressing: the lab's reference, then the port with fewer tiles (at the same settings the port is pixel-identical; see tests/multigrid.test.ts)
