3D print

Turns a height map into a closed solid: white is high, black is low. Use the current design (its relief map: letters highest, then bands, patterns and ground), or any image, such as a drawing, a logo or an exported PNG. Flat levels trace each plateau's outline between pixels, for smooth curves and straight walls; every grey follows the image's shading, for photographs.
